Hao-Yu Tsai (蔡皓宇) received his B.S. degree in Interdisciplinary Studies (Mathematics and Computer Science) and his Ph.D. in Communications Engineering from National Tsing Hua University in 2022, and 2026, respectively. His research interests broadly include **intelligence systems**, **optimization**, **wireless communications**, and **computational photography**.
